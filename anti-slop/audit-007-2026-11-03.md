# Reading board: measured pen.dev comparison

Scope: refine the existing `polish-reading-board` implementation after comparing its production render with `design/design.pen`. Direction: the existing pen.dev card table; no random style draw or seed. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 2. Remix 3 `css`/`mix`, Bun, approved artwork, and the reading engine are retained.

## Design read

This is an interactive reading screen, so the design-taste app-screen workflow applies. The user's pen.dev direction takes priority over the skill's generic type preferences. The screen answers: what should I ask, choose, place, and reveal next?

Its own material: Pseudo Tarot; sixteen Developer Tarot cards; nine hidden choices; Context / Problem / Advice; engraved teal backs and fronts; amber actions; Newsreader titles; Funnel Sans body text; IBM Plex Mono labels; manual card reveals; consent before sharing; three compact phone cards with full-width interpretations. No sample customers, numbers, testimonials, or new imagery were added.

Idea: the three positions in a developer's decision become a physical card table. The live thing is the existing question / placement / reveal ritual. The blocks answer question, spread, choice, and verdict in that order.

## Inspector gate

Final `inspect.mjs --app` on production: **0 FAIL** at 1440, 768, and 390 px. Final lint of the nine rendered view modules: **0 FAIL**, eight monospace warnings. The initial broad source scan also found a Unicode emoji in a test fixture; it is validation input, not displayed UI, and was kept.

Explicit inspector allowances: `micro-label,font-count`. Pen.dev specifies uppercase labels and the three brand faces; removing them would contradict the requested reference. No contrast, overflow, clipping, or fallback-font check was disabled.

Retained warnings, with reasons:

- `palette-cream`: the measured background is dark `#0A0D0C`; the flagged cream is the approved text `#F3EDDF`.
- `unused-canvas`: the heuristic mistakes the widened header for a sidebar; the actual 828 px spread has equal 306 px outer margins at 1440.
- `panel-void`: these are intentionally empty card-placement targets with a centered position and bottom hint, not stretched report panels.
- `app-title-size`: the 36 px Newsreader prompt is specified by the desktop reference.
- `tiny-text`: phone secondary labels remain 11–12 px to match the compact card table; the question and interpretation text are 16 px. No text is below 11 px.
