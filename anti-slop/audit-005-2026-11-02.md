Expected invalid input returns controlled failures; shared URLs are public editable data and carry no authenticity or encryption claim.

## Existing findings retained

## Coverage limits

Chromium and emulated touch were checked. Clipboard success/denial were simulated to verify app behavior; the system clipboard was not overwritten. Physical devices, Firefox, Safari, CI, the production container, and deployment/proxy logging were not checked. This is a scoped feature check, not a full accessibility or infrastructure audit.
